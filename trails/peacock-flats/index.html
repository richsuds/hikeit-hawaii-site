Peacock Flats

Moderate · about 2 miles one way on Mokulēʻia Trail to the forest reserve boundary.

Weather, UV and mud readings may be unavailable. Check official conditions before visiting.

Bring a printed day-use permit; 4WD is required on forest roads. Overnight camping requires a separate permit.

Drive in via the Kaʻena Point Satellite Tracking Station checkpoint from Keawaʻula, not the north-side Mokulēʻia Forest Reserve access road, which is closed to public vehicles. The checkpoint may refuse entry to non-U.S. citizens. Check access before driving: (808) 697-4311.
