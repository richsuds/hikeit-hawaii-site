Diamond Head Summit Trail

Plan the Diamond Head hike with round-trip distance, entry and parking reservations, the Waikīkī bus approach, terrain and official access sources.

Also known as: Diamond Head Trail · Lēʻahi Summit Trail · Leahi Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Honolulu
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Length: 1.6 miles round trip (0.8 mile each way)
  • Elevation gain: 560 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Division of State Parks
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

1½–2 hours round trip

Access planning

Non-Hawaiʻi residents need advance entry reservations. Reserve parking separately if using the monument lot; walk-in and drop-off visitors need entry only. Confirm the current rules and access notices before visiting.

Additional listing notice: Admission is controlled; reservations are required for non-Hawaiʻi residents.

Permit & access details · Official trail information

Arrival and parking

State Parks locates the entrance off Diamond Head Road between Makapuʻu Avenue and 18th Avenue. The summit trail starts beside the parking lot inside the crater. A bus-stop or outside drop-off approach adds walking before the trailhead. Follow official entrance instructions and your entry/parking reservation; no unverified driving pin is supplied here.

Plan your visit

The 1.6-mile return hike starts at the crater-floor trailhead. Walking from a bus stop or an outside drop-off adds distance and time.

Book the right entry

Non-Hawaiʻi residents need advance entry reservations. Add parking only if you will use the monument lot; walk-in and drop-off visitors need entry only. State Parks lists $5 entry per nonresident and $10 per nonresident vehicle; children age three and under enter free. Check the booking total and current rules before paying.

State Parks fees and reservations · Official reservation FAQ

Fit the hike around your reservation

Normal hours are 6 am–6 pm, with last entry at 4:30 pm for the 4 pm reservation. The park closes on Christmas and New Year’s Day. If parking inside, leave by the end of your parking reservation; every visitor must exit through the tunnel by 6 pm. Allow extra time for the approach, queues and breaks.

Park hours · Entry and exit requirements

Choose the terrain, not just the view

The route gains about 560 feet, with uneven ground, steep stairs, a tunnel and exposed sections. Bring water, sun protection and footwear with grip. Compare your group’s comfort with stairs and heat before reserving; short mileage does not make this a level stroll.

Official route and terrain

Coming from Waikīkī without a car

TheBus’s visitor reference lists routes 2, 2L and 200 for Diamond Head Crater. Check the route serving your actual starting point, travel date and direction. The stop-to-entrance walk is additional to the summit hike; allow time to reach the entrance within your reservation. An entry reservation is still required for nonresidents arriving by bus.

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 1.6-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.
