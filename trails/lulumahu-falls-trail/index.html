Lulumahu Falls Trail

Plan Lulumahu Falls Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Lulumahu Falls · Lulumahu Waterfall Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Nuʻuanu
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — DOFAW
  • Trail type: WATERFALL
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Waterfall, Stream, Historic infrastructure

Amenities

No maintained visitor facilities

Route orientation

Listed start: Permit-authorized access. Listed end: Lulumahu Falls. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Flash flooding, stream crossings, mud, navigation

Access planning

DLNR hiking permit required.

Plan for the terrain

Water levels can change quickly after rain, including rain upstream. Do not enter fast-moving water or rely on a waterfall-flow estimate to judge a crossing.

Published route length is unavailable here; check the official route before planning your time. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
