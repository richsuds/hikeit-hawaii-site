Lāʻie Falls Trail

Plan Lāʻie Falls Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Laie Falls Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Koʻolauloa / North Shore
  • Difficulty: Intermediate; challenging waterfall descent
  • Length: 3.1 miles
  • Managed by: Hawaii Reserves, Inc.
  • Trail type: WATERFALL
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Waterfall, Stream, Scenic views, Ridge

Amenities

Permit required

Mapped hazards

Landslide damage, slippery footing, steep waterfall descent, stream conditions

Access planning

A Hawaii Reserves hiking permit is required. Current landslide restrictions prohibit travel beyond Pine Forest.

Conditions note: Weather, mud and water-flow information are planning estimates, not safety ratings. Check official alerts, closures and access requirements before visiting.
