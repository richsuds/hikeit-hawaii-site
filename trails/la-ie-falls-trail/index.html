Lāʻie Falls Trail

Plan Lāʻie Falls Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Laie Falls Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Koʻolauloa / North Shore
  • Difficulty: Intermediate; challenging waterfall descent
  • Length: 3.1 miles
  • Managed by: Hawaii Reserves, Inc.
  • Trail type: WATERFALL
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Waterfall, Stream, Scenic views, Ridge

Amenities

Permit required

Route orientation

Listed start: Lāʻie Park / Poohaili Street access. Listed end: Lāʻie Falls (access currently restricted). Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Landslide damage, slippery footing, steep waterfall descent, stream conditions

Access planning

A Hawaii Reserves hiking permit is required. Current landslide restrictions prohibit travel beyond Pine Forest.

Plan for the terrain

Water levels can change quickly after rain, including rain upstream. Do not enter fast-moving water or rely on a waterfall-flow estimate to judge a crossing.

The listed 3.1-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
