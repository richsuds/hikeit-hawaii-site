ʻEhukai Pillbox Trail

Plan ʻEhukai Pillbox Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Ehukai Pillbox Trail · Sunset Pillbox Trail · Sunset Pillbox Hike · Sunset Pillboxes · Peace Hike · North Shore Pillbox

Trail facts

  • Region: North Shore / Sunset
  • Difficulty: Moderate; short, steep and exposed
  • Length: 2.3 miles
  • Managed by: Access authority not confirmed
  • Trail type: RIDGE
  • Access status: VERIFY

Trail features

Historic pillbox, North Shore views, Sunset views, Ocean views, Banzai Pipeline views

Amenities

No maintained trail facilities

Route orientation

Listed start: Access near Sunset Beach Elementary School. Listed end: ʻEhukai pillbox ridge viewpoints. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

High UV, heat, steep or muddy footing, exposed slopes, route finding, descending after dark

Access planning

Verify current access, school-area parking restrictions, and posted signs before visiting.

Plan for the terrain

Ridgelines can expose hikers to wind, sun, steep drop-offs and slippery footing after rain. Keep to the established route and turn around when conditions change.

The listed 2.3-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
