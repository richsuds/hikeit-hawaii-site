Kīlauea Iki Trail

Plan Kīlauea Iki Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Kilauea Iki Trail · Kilauea Iki Spur

Trail facts

  • Region: Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park
  • Difficulty: MODERATE
  • Length: 2.2 miles
  • Managed by: National Park Service — Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: VERIFY

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 2.2-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
