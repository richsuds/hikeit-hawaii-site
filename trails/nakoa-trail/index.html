Nakoa Trail

Plan Nakoa Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Nā Koa Trail · Kahana Nakoa Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Windward / Kahana
  • Difficulty: Easy trail; longer approach
  • Length: 5 miles
  • Elevation gain: 400 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Division of State Parks
  • Trail type: STREAM
  • Access status: VERIFY

Trail features

Koa forest, Kahana Stream crossings, Cultural landscape

Amenities

Orientation Center and park facilities

Route orientation

Listed start: Orientation Center. Listed end: Nakoa forest loop. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Mud, two stream crossings, rain

Access planning

No hiking permit required; check current park hours and notices.

Official trail information

Arrival and parking

Driving directions are unavailable until a road-accessible arrival point is verified. A mapped route point is not verified parking. Check the land manager’s entry, parking and transport instructions before travel.

Official arrival and access instructions

Plan for the terrain

Water levels can change quickly after rain, including rain upstream. Do not enter fast-moving water or rely on a waterfall-flow estimate to judge a crossing.

The listed 5-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
