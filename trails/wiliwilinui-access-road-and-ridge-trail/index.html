Wiliwilinui Access Road and Ridge Trail

Plan Wiliwilinui Access Road and Ridge Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Trail facts

  • Region: Honolulu
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Length: 3 miles
  • Elevation range: 1600 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Nā Ala Hele
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Estimated hiking time

2½–4 hours round trip

The access manager’s estimate starts at the trailhead. Add the gate-to-trailhead walk and return if parking outside the community or using rideshare. This estimate does not grant access. Access planning

Community gate access is limited to sunrise–sunset. Vehicle parking passes are limited; follow all association rules.

Arrival and parking

Driving directions are unavailable until a road-accessible arrival point is verified. A mapped route point is not verified parking. Check the land manager’s entry, parking and transport instructions before travel.

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 3-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
