Kuaokala Trail

Plan Kuaokala Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Kuaokala Trail · Kuaokalā Trail · Kuaokalā–Peacock Flats Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Waiʻanae / West Oʻahu
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Length: 2 miles
  • Elevation range: 500 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — DOFAW
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Hike, Nature Study, Hunt

Amenities

Parking, camp, Table, Shelter

Route orientation

Listed start: Forest Reserve Road. Listed end: Mokuleia firebreak (Mauka) Rd.. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Blind corner, cliff, footing, fire, hunters

Access planning

A DLNR permit is required for vehicle access through the Kaʻena Point Satellite Tracking Station road. A separate camping permit is required for Peacock Flats. Verify current access announcements before departure.

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 2-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
