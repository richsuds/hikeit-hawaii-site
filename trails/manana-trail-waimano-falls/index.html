Manana Trail / Waimano Falls

Plan Manana Trail / Waimano Falls with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Waimano Falls Trail · Manana Ridge Trail

Trail facts

  • Region: Ewa
  • Difficulty: Difficult
  • Length: 6 miles
  • Elevation range: 1700 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Nā Ala Hele
  • Trail type: WATERFALL
  • Access status: VERIFY

Trail features

Hike, Nature Study, Hunt, Archery, Bio. Sens.

Amenities

Camping, Parking Shelters

Route orientation

Listed start: End of Komo Mai Drive. Listed end: Koolau Ridge. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Hunters, Footing, Cliffs, Fire, Narrow Trail, Sun

Plan for the terrain

Water levels can change quickly after rain, including rain upstream. Do not enter fast-moving water or rely on a waterfall-flow estimate to judge a crossing.

The listed 6-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
