Kamananui Valley Road

Plan Kamananui Valley Road with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Trail facts

  • Region: Honolulu
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Length: 4 miles
  • Elevation range: 600 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Nā Ala Hele
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Hike, Nature Study, Hunt, Stream, Archeo, Culture

Amenities

Camp, Parking, Toilets, Water, Tables, Trash

Route orientation

Listed start: End of Ala Aolani Road-park. Listed end: Rd. turns trail at 2.5-ends at 4.0. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Blind Corner, Footing, Flash Floods, Hunters

Access planning

Private or time-limited access is recorded in the official trail dataset. Verify current access before visiting.

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 4-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
