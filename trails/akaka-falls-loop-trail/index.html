ʻAkaka Falls Loop Trail

Plan ʻAkaka Falls Loop Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Trail facts

  • Region: North Hāmākua Coast
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Length: 0.4 miles
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Division of State Parks
  • Trail type: WATERFALL
  • Access status: VERIFY

Trail features

Paved tropical loop with viewpoints for 442-foot ʻAkaka Falls and 300-foot Kahuna Falls.

Amenities

Parking and park facilities; entrance and parking fees apply to nonresidents.

Mapped hazards

Frequent rain, wet pavement, stairs, short slopes, and steep gorge edges.

Plan for the terrain

Water levels can change quickly after rain, including rain upstream. Do not enter fast-moving water or rely on a waterfall-flow estimate to judge a crossing.

The listed 0.4-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
