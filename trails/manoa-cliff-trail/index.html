Manoa Cliff Trail

Plan Manoa Cliff Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Trail facts

  • Region: Honolulu
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Length: 2.3 miles
  • Elevation range: 500 feet
  • Managed by: Hawaiʻi DLNR — Nā Ala Hele
  • Trail type: STANDARD
  • Access status: VERIFY

Trail features

Hike, Nature Study, Bio. Sens.

Amenities

Parking, Bridge, Bench, Interpretive Signs

Route orientation

Listed start: Round Top Drive. Listed end: Tantalus Drive. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Cliff, Footing, Hunters, Landslides, Narrow Trail

Plan for the terrain

Recent rain can leave steps, roots and rock slick after skies clear. Consider heat, shade, footing and the return trip as well as the forecast.

The listed 2.3-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Access has not been verified; confirm the current status with the land manager.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
