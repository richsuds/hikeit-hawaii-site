Kolekole Pass Trail

Plan Kolekole Pass Trail with its official route map, difficulty, access details, weather, UV, rainfall, and estimated mud and water-flow conditions.

Also known as: Kolekole Trail · Kolekole Hiking Trail · Kolekole Pass

Trail facts

  • Region: Central Oʻahu / Schofield
  • Difficulty: Moderate / Medium
  • Length: 1 miles
  • Managed by: U.S. Army Garrison Hawaiʻi
  • Trail type: RIDGE
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Waiʻanae Range views, historic pass, scenic overlook

Amenities

Scenic overlook bench

Route orientation

Listed start: End of Trimble Road, Schofield Barracks. Listed end: Bench and scenic overlook. Confirm the actual route and return path with the land manager's current map.

Mapped hazards

Steep areas, unstable rocks, steep drops, poor visibility, military training closures

Access planning

DoD identification or an approved visitor pass is required. Verify an announced open date before visiting.

Plan for the terrain

Ridgelines can expose hikers to wind, sun, steep drop-offs and slippery footing after rain. Keep to the established route and turn around when conditions change.

The listed 1-mile length does not account for detours, rests or the time needed to return. Check for new closures or restrictions before travel.

Check before you leave

  1. Read the latest National Weather Service forecast and alerts for the trail area.
  2. Confirm the manager’s current access and permit rules, including the intended entry point.
  3. Share your route and expected return time with someone, and choose a lower-exposure alternative if conditions change.
