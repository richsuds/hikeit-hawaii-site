Trail facts

  • Region: Central Oʻahu / Schofield
  • Difficulty: Moderate / Medium
  • Length: 1 miles
  • Managed by: U.S. Army Garrison Hawaiʻi
  • Trail type: RIDGE
  • Access status: RESTRICTED

Trail features

Waiʻanae Range views, historic pass, scenic overlook

Amenities

Scenic overlook bench

Mapped hazards

Access planning

DoD identification or an approved visitor pass is required. Verify an announced open date before visiting.

Conditions note: Weather, mud and water-flow information are planning estimates, not safety ratings. Check official alerts, closures and access requirements before visiting.
