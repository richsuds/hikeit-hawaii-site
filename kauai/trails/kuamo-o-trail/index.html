Access planning

Kuamoʻo Trail closed until further notice because of bridge damage.

DOFAW’s official Kuamoʻo Trail listing closes the trail until further notice because of structural bridge damage. DLNR’s November 8, 2026 recovery notice also lists Kuamoʻo Bridge as closed. Do not enter or attempt a bypass. This restriction does not establish the current status of the separate Nounou East or West routes; check their own notices.
