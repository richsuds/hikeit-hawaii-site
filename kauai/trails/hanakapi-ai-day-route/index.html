Route scope

This listing covers Hāʻena to Hanakāpīʻai stream and beach, about 4 miles round trip. The separate falls extension is about 8 miles round trip. Go Hāʻena describes these hikes as very difficult, with steep terrain and flash-flood exposure. These route facts do not establish current access. Check current State Parks notices and verify today’s access before travel.

State Parks route distances · Go Hāʻena hiking guidance

Non-Hawaiʻi residents need an advance Hāʻena entry reservation, plus parking if using the park lot, or a shuttle reservation that includes entry. Hawaiʻi residents with valid identification are exempt from day-use reservations; parking is limited. A Nāpali Coast camping permit is required to continue beyond Hanakāpīʻai.

Arrival and parking

The route is reached through Hāʻena State Park at Kēʻē. The former inland map pin is not a trailhead or parking destination. Driving directions are withheld until a road-accessible arrival point is verified. Use official entry, parking and shuttle instructions; do not park on the highway shoulder.
