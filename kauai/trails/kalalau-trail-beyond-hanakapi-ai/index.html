Official route facts

Difficult · 22 miles round trip

Full route from Kēʻē to Kalalau Beach: 11 miles one way, 22 miles round trip. This is not the distance from Hanakāpīʻai to Kalalau.

A valid Nāpali Coast State Wilderness Park camping permit is required for anyone proceeding beyond Hanakāpīʻai, even for a day hike. Confirm current closures and parking or transport arrangements before travel.
