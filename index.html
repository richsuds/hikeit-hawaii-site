Four Hawaiʻi hikes, four different outings

Choose a paved coast walk, a bamboo forest, a canyon outing or a volcanic crater. Check the trail page and its official source for current conditions and access.

  • Oʻahu: Makapuʻu Point Lighthouse Trail

    A paved uphill walk with ocean views. The route is exposed to sun and wind.

  • Maui: Pīpīwai Trail

    A forest hike through bamboo to a waterfall viewpoint in Haleakalā National Park.

  • Kauaʻi: Waimea Canyon Trail

    A canyon outing from Kōkeʻe. Check park notices before driving up.

  • Hawaiʻi Island: Kīlauea Iki Trail

    Walk from rainforest to the solidified lava lake on the crater floor, then climb back out.

How to use the information

Check the forecast for the trail area, recent rain and stream observations where available, the land manager’s access notices, and your own group’s experience. Conditions may change faster than a forecast or model can update.
