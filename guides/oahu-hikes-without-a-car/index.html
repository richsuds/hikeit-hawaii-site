Oʻahu Hikes from Waikīkī Without a Rental Car

Start with the whole journey, not just the trail mileage. Diamond Head and Mānoa Falls offer different outings from Honolulu, but both need an approach plan, time for the return and a current access check. These route references are starting points, not a door-to-door timetable.

Diamond Head Summit Trail

Estimated hiking time: 1½–2 hours round trip.

State Parks guidance for the designated summit route and return. State Parks time guidance.

TheBus’s Waikīkī visitor reference lists routes 2, 2L and 200 for Diamond Head Crater. Confirm which serves your starting point and date, then include the walk to the park entrance and crater-floor trailhead. The summit hike is 1.6 miles round trip, with stairs and about 560 feet of climbing.

TheBus Waikīkī visitor reference · Official trail approach and distance

Access to plan around

Nonresident walk-ins and drop-off visitors need an entry reservation. A parking reservation is needed only when using the monument lot. Normal park hours and your reservation govern the visit; a bus arrival does not guarantee entry.

Mānoa Falls Trail

Estimated hiking time: About 1½ hours round trip.

Based on a local operator’s guided waterfall walk, not an official average. Allow longer for mud, crowds and your group’s pace. Local operator’s hiking-time estimate.

Route 5 links Ala Moana with Mānoa/Kumuone; check your connection from Waikīkī. Lyon Arboretum’s directions describe walking from the final stop toward Paradise Park and the arboretum, past the Mānoa Falls trailhead. That neighborhood walk is additional to the hike. Verify current pedestrian access, stops and return service.

Official Route 5 description · Lyon’s public-transit directions

Access to plan around

Check Nā Ala Hele for current trail access. Follow barriers and closure signs. Lyon Arboretum’s grounds and parking are separate from Mānoa Falls; arboretum parking is not available to falls hikers. Do not use a waterfall-flow estimate to judge swimming or stream-crossing safety.

Check both directions before choosing a hike

Use TheBus’s current routes and schedules for your travel date, starting stop, transfers and return. Allow time for missed connections, traffic and the walk between the stop and the legal trail entrance. Do not plan around the final service of the day or assume a rideshare will be available after a remote hike.

Bus access does not replace a reservation

Book Diamond Head entry before arranging your day. Nonresident walk-ins need entry reservations, while visitors using the monument lot also need parking. Match your arrival to the reservation and allow time to walk from the bus stop. Everyone must exit the crater by 6 pm.

Diamond Head reservation FAQ

Keep Makapuʻu Beach separate from the lighthouse trail

TheBus visitor reference lists a route to Makapuʻu Beach. That does not verify a pedestrian approach to the lighthouse trailhead. This guide does not recommend a highway-shoulder walk or use the beach stop as a substitute trailhead. Choose an outing whose final walking approach you can confirm.

Official attraction-level bus reference

Budget the approach, hike and return together

The hiking estimates below do not include hotel-to-stop travel, waiting, transfers or the extra walk to the trail. Check fares with TheBus and entry charges with the land manager. Carry water and sun or rain protection, and shorten or change the outing when the return no longer fits daylight and park hours.
