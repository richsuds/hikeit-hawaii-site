How to choose

Easy is a route classification, not a promise about today's footing or your group's ability. Check distance, climbing, shade, heat, toilets, reservations, and the latest access notice. Turn around if the surface or weather exceeds your plan.

A paved or managed path differs from a short natural trail: steps, loose rock, roots, mud, and exposed sun can matter more than mileage. Compare the individual trail facts and the official manager's current rules before leaving.
