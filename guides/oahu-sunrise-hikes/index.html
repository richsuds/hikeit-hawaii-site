Oʻahu Sunrise Hikes & Morning Views

The sun rises on the eastern side of the sky, but an east-facing view is only one part of a sunrise plan. The date, local ridges, clouds and the time you may legally enter all determine what is possible.

Strenuous • experienced hikers

Koko Crater Railway Trail

Estimated hiking time: About 2–4 hours round trip.

A published visitor-guide estimate, not City guidance. The steep ascent and careful descent vary widely with fitness, heat and breaks; do not use this range to promise a sunrise arrival. Independent guide’s time estimate.

A possible sunrise plan for fit, experienced hikers who can manage the steep ascent in low light. Match your actual pace to sunrise and allow for breaks; darkness makes footing harder to assess. An after-sunrise climb is an alternative if you are unfamiliar with the route.

Access to plan around

The City lists Koko Head District Park closed 11 pm–4 am. That opening window is not a guarantee of trail conditions or parking availability. Confirm current City notices, legal parking and the return plan before leaving.

After-opening morning alternative

Makapuʻu Point Lighthouse Trail

HikeIt difficulty: Easy. Paved with a sustained uphill grade, about 500 feet of gain and little shade. Heat and wind add effort. State Parks rates the route Moderate.

Estimated hiking time: About 2 hours round trip.

State Parks allows time to enjoy the views on the main paved route. Closed tide pools and the lighthouse are not part of this outing. State Parks time guidance.

A coastal walk for morning views once access is permitted. Visibility and wind can change the experience; the sunrise location alone does not establish a suitable hiking time.

Access to plan around

Kaiwi publishes 7 am opening, with seasonal closing times. Verify current notices and obey posted restrictions.

Time-dependent early visit

Diamond Head Summit Trail

Estimated hiking time: 1½–2 hours round trip.

State Parks guidance for the designated summit route and return. State Parks time guidance.

Compare your reservation, legal entry time and the climb with the sunrise time on your travel date. An early reservation does not guarantee reaching the summit before sunrise.

Access to plan around

Is Makapuʻu a good sunrise location?

Its southeastern coastal setting makes morning light a natural reason to consider Makapuʻu. However, the published Kaiwi opening time is 7 am. This guide does not establish permission to enter on foot before opening or endorse roadside parking to bypass a gate. Plan a walk after opening unless the land manager explicitly confirms an authorized earlier visit.

East is a starting point, not a fixed bearing

The sunrise position shifts through the year; it is not due east every morning. A headland or ridge can also hide the horizon from a particular viewpoint. Check the sunrise time for your date and the view from the actual destination, then add the approach time within permitted hours.

Make a morning plan that works

If legal opening is after sunrise, enjoy the morning coastline instead of promising yourself the sunrise moment. Confirm parking and reservations the day before, and check wind and rain again before departure. Do not extend a route onto an unofficial ridge or a closed shoreline to find a better angle.

Check again before you go

Hours and reservations are planning facts, not a live opening confirmation. Check State Parks notices, the National Weather Service Honolulu forecast and the manager’s page for your destination.

Temporary notices reviewed November 1, 2026: State Parks lists storm-recovery closures and facility impacts around Hawaiʻi, including an ʻAiea Loop restroom closure through November 25. Makapuʻu Lookout repair work is separate from the Lighthouse Trail. Recheck the official notice because listed end dates are not reopening guarantees.
