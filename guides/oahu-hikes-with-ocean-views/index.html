How to choose

Coastal and ridge views often mean limited shade and greater exposure to sun and wind. Rain can make steep dirt, rock, and steps slippery even when the destination is clear. Stay on established routes and away from cliff edges.

Compare paved coastal viewpoints with ridgeline routes by elevation gain, surface, and access—not only by the view. Start early when appropriate, carry water, and check the trail's official status and weather shortly before departure.
