How to choose

A listing here signals that access needs checking; it does not mean a permit is always available or that the trail is open. Rules can depend on the route, date, entry point, land manager, and military or private-land restrictions.

Open the official permit or access page for the specific trail, confirm the date and entry point, and keep any required reservation or authorization with you. If the source is unclear, contact the land manager before traveling.
